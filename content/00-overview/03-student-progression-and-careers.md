# Student Progression and Careers

*Digital Software Development T Level → Overview → Progression and Careers*

This page covers who this Technical Qualification is designed for, the job roles it leads to, and the routes students can take after completing it.

## Student profile

Students undertaking this Technical Qualification are 16-19 years old and in full-time education. The typical student:

- has a clear idea about the industry sector they want a career in;
- has an idea of the type of job role they'd like to explore as a career.

## Career pathways

This Technical Qualification aligns to the **Level 3 Apprenticeship for Software Development Technicians**, so it supports progression straight into entry-level software development job opportunities. Job roles it can lead to include:

- Software Development Technician
- Junior Developer
- Junior Web Developer
- Junior Application Developer
- Junior Mobile App Developer
- Junior Games Developer
- Junior Software Developer
- Junior Application Support Analyst
- Junior Programmer
- Assistant Programmer
- Automated Test Developer

## Progression routes

Students have three broad options after completing the T Level:

1. **Apprenticeship** - progress to the Level 3 Software Development Technician Apprenticeship (or similar), to develop and gain certification of full occupational competence, or later to a higher-level Apprenticeship such as the Level 4 Software Developer standard, depending on skills and experience.
2. **Higher education** - progress to a relevant Higher National Certificate (HNC), Higher National Diploma (HND) or degree programme, for students who don't have access to an Apprenticeship or prefer a more academic route. Students must check entry requirements for each degree programme with the relevant higher education provider.
3. **Direct employment** - move straight into one of the entry-level job roles listed above, since the T Level is designed to deliver threshold competence for the sector.

## Key terms

- **Threshold competence** - the level of knowledge and skill needed to be workplace-ready and continue developing on the job, short of full occupational competence.
- **Level 3 / Level 4 Apprenticeship** - the natural next step(s) after this T Level, building toward full occupational competence and, eventually, more senior standards like the Level 4 Software Developer Apprenticeship.
